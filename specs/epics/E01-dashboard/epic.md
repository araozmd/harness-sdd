# Epic E01 — Dashboard

## Business brief
The operator/admin landing surface: an at-a-glance view of conversation volume, bot
performance, and items needing attention, with navigation into deeper screens
(including the handoff inbox from E02).

## Success criteria (epic level)
- An operator sees, within one screen, how many conversations are active, bot-handled,
  and awaiting a human.
- Key metrics load fast and are accurate to the underlying data.
