# Harness rationale and deletion ledger — Functional Spec

## Context
The repository explains what its harness does, but a newcomer still has to infer
why a harness is preferable to repeatedly prompting a capable model. Maintainers
also lack a durable way to distinguish scaffolding that compensates for current
model limitations from process controls that preserve human intent, evidence,
and accountability. This feature gives both audiences one cold-readable rationale
and a deletion ledger that makes every notable mechanism's retention hypothesis
explicit and testable.

## Business rules
- The rationale uses a two-layer decision model:
  **capability compensation** exists primarily because current models or agent
  runtimes exhibit a measured failure mode; **durable trust and intent** preserves
  a human or engineering property that increased model capability alone does not
  remove.
- The classification is by a mechanism's primary purpose, not by whether it uses
  code, prose, or a model. Mixed mechanisms are placed in one primary layer and
  their retained durable property is named in the removal criterion.
- "Compensation" means a candidate for controlled simplification, not deprecated
  or safe to delete now. A removal requires a representative, repeatable
  before/after evaluation with predeclared quality and safety thresholds; anecdote,
  benchmark movement alone, or a vendor model launch is insufficient.
- A durable mechanism may still be redesigned or replaced. Its removal requires
  evidence that the protected intent, accountability, integrity, portability, or
  verification property is no longer required or is preserved by a simpler
  replacement. A stronger model by itself is not that evidence.
- The ledger baseline is the notable mechanisms exposed by `AGENTS.md`,
  `README.md`, `docs/HARNESS.md`, `docs/WORKFLOW.md`,
  `harness.config.yaml`, ADR-0001, and the installed-body lifecycle at the time
  this feature is implemented. Closely coupled submechanisms may share one row,
  but none of the baseline rows named in R4–R5 may be omitted.
- External publications are attributed as evidence for particular observations,
  not presented as proof of universal industry consensus. Product or benchmark
  results retain their stated model, task, date, and study-scope limitations.
- The rationale and ledger are guidance only. They do not remove a mechanism,
  change a role contract, alter a gate, add runtime parsing, or create a
  machine-readable policy surface.

## Architecture alignment
`specs/architecture.md` is absent, so there is no complete planning-tier
architecture document to consume. The repository does contain the accepted
selection decision below, which the ledger directly touches.

- **ADR-0001 — next() selection moves from prose routing to a deterministic
  tool:** the ledger classifies the current prose interpretation of `next()` as
  capability compensation and records E16-F03's deterministic replacement as
  the evidence-backed exit path; it does not implement, revise, or pre-empt that
  decision.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)
> Each requirement is one observable, testable behavior.

- **R1** — When a reader opens `docs/RATIONALE.md` without prior repository
  context, the document shall define an agent harness, explain why repeated
  prompting alone is insufficient for this project's target work, identify the
  intended users, and state that the harness's value shifts from capability
  support toward specification, verification, and trust as models improve.
- **R2** — The rationale shall define the capability-compensation and durable
  trust-and-intent layers, shall give an operational rule for classifying a
  mixed mechanism by primary purpose, and shall state that neither
  "compensation" nor "durable" means immutable.
- **R3** — The deletion ledger shall be a human-first Markdown table in the same
  `docs/RATIONALE.md`, with exactly one row per listed mechanism or explicitly
  named mechanism group and columns for mechanism, layer, present purpose,
  evidence required to reconsider or remove it, and repository pointers.
- **R4** — The compensation portion of the ledger shall explicitly inventory:
  structural preflight in `init.sh`; curated clean contexts, reset threshold,
  and file handoffs; role-scoped `DO NOT TOUCH` and minimal-tool instructions;
  read-only Scout discovery; Doc-critic advisory passes; the Reviewer's
  cross-file consistency audit; multi-round Builder↔Reviewer correction; and the
  current prose interpretation of `next()`.
- **R5** — The durable portion of the ledger shall explicitly inventory:
  EARS specs and R-id traceability; file-backed TaskStore/spec/progress history;
  role separation and independent review; human approval/autonomy gates;
  deterministic verification and behavioral evidence; board locking and
  worktree isolation; dependency diagnostics and deterministic selection;
  architecture/ADR alignment and epic drift checks; telemetry; ownership and
  scoped selection; umbrella contracts, slice rollups, and integration gates;
  store/execution/mirror seams with local state as source of truth; and
  repository portability, configuration layering, versioned installation.
- **R6** — Each ledger row shall state a mechanism-specific, observable
  re-test/removal criterion and shall default to retention when that evidence is
  absent; durable rows shall name the protected property a replacement must
  preserve.
- **R7** — The prose-routing ledger row shall cite ADR-0001, shall state that the
  mechanism remains in force until E16-F03 is reviewed and shipped, and shall
  distinguish deleting model interpretation of routing from deleting the
  human-auditable gate policy.
- **R8** — Where the rationale makes an externally derived claim, it shall cite
  the primary publisher page and bound the claim to that source's reported
  system and setting; its source notes shall include Anthropic's long-running
  harness work, OpenAI's Codex harness-engineering report, LangChain's harness
  verification report, Thoughtworks' feedback-sensor guidance, and METR's
  early-2025 experienced-open-source-developer study without generalizing the
  METR result beyond that study.
- **R9** — The repository `README.md` shall link to `docs/RATIONALE.md` from its
  introductory "why" path and shall list `RATIONALE` in the documented `docs/`
  layout.
- **R10** — The repository `AGENTS.md` docs map and `docs/HARNESS.md` shall link
  to `docs/RATIONALE.md` without duplicating the ledger or increasing the
  always-loaded entrypoint beyond a concise pointer.
- **R11** — When `harness-install.sh` performs a fresh install, the installed
  `.harness/docs/RATIONALE.md` shall exist and be byte-identical to the source
  document through the installer's existing whole-`docs/` copy path.
- **R12** — The rationale and ledger shall remain usable as text-only portable
  Markdown: every distinction shall be stated in words, links shall use
  descriptive labels, tables shall have headers, and no color, image, Mermaid,
  script, or generated rendering shall be required to understand the content.
- **R13** — The implementation deliverables shall change no runtime behavior,
  TaskStore schema or status semantics, gate, selection outcome, role
  responsibility, or external API, and shall add no runtime parser, dependency,
  machine-readable ledger, or deletion automation; normal Orchestrator-owned
  feature-status and history bookkeeping remains permitted and out of Builder
  scope.
- **R14** — Because the new rationale and its navigation are installed-body
  additions, `VERSION` shall advance by exactly one SemVer MINOR step with patch
  zero and `CHANGELOG.md` shall contain a matching E16-F02 entry.
- **R15** — The permanent verification suite and mandatory Reviewer checks shall
  provide complete R1–R14 traceability, shall verify the installed-document
  requirements in a freshly installed layout, shall resolve every local Markdown
  link introduced by this feature, and shall append (not replace) the focused
  suite in the configured full test command.

## Out of scope
- Removing, weakening, or changing any harness mechanism or role contract.
- Implementing E16-F03's deterministic selector or revising ADR-0001.
- A YAML/JSON ledger, schema, parser, score, automatic expiry, scheduled model
  benchmark, or deletion command.
- A general literature review, a claim that cited organizations agree on one
  architecture, or reproduction of source text beyond short labels and
  paraphrases.
- Redesigning `docs/HARNESS.md`, adding a documentation generator/site, or
  introducing a new docs index beyond the repository's existing maps.

## Open questions
None blocking. The intake choices are resolved as follows:
- One `docs/RATIONALE.md` keeps the two-layer explanation adjacent to the ledger
  it governs. Two sibling documents would create cross-file drift without adding
  a distinct audience or lifecycle.
- A human-first Markdown table is selected over YAML. There is no approved
  consumer for machine-readable data, and adding one would turn guidance into a
  compatibility contract.
- `docs/HARNESS.md` remains the short conceptual introduction and points readers
  to the deeper rationale rather than duplicating or replacing it.
