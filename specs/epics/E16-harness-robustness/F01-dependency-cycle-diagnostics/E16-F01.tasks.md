## Mandatory Reviewer behavioral gate
Before approval, the Reviewer shall run a clean Orchestrator-role dry run against
disposable TaskStore/config fixtures for R8–R13 and write the captured output plus
before/after board hashes to
`progress/E16-F01-review-roundN/no-selection-behavior.md`. The scenarios must
cover: draft epic; missing, non-done, cyclic, and done-but-unmerged dependencies;
both human gates; resolved owned, other-owned, and unowned `--mine` candidates;
empty, failed `@me`/`self`, and bare selection identity cases; and empty/all-done
boards. The Reviewer shall verify exact record/detail/summary text, deterministic
ordering, scoped-only ownership reasons, informational completion, and
byte-identical fixture state. Missing or contradictory evidence is a rejection.
