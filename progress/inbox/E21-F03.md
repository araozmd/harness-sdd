# Intent — `/sdd-pr-loop`: per-round finding trend + "split, don't re-review" at the round cap

## Problem / who
`pr_loop.max_rounds` (default 4) caps the loop and labels the PR `needs-human`. What it does
not do is say **what the human is supposed to conclude**. In practice the conclusion drawn is
"run it again": on `viernes-bookings-api` PR #76 the operator posted `@codex review` twelve
times by hand. Rounds 5–12 cost roughly 2M input tokens and 8 hours of wall clock to keep
rediscovering that the diff was too large to review in one pass.

The information that would have ended it after round 4 was already on the PR and nobody
aggregated it — the per-round blocking-finding count:

```
R1  R2  R3  R4  R5  R6  R7  R8  R9  R10 R11 R12
 1   3   1   2   1   3   1   2   2   1   2   1     (P1 only; 20 P1 + 15 P2 total)
```

A *decaying* count means the review is converging and one more round is rational. A **flat**
count means the reviewer is sampling a surface larger than one pass can cover, and more rounds
buy more samples, not more confidence. Those two situations look identical at the cap today.

## Success outcome
At the round cap the loop reports the trend it actually measured and names the right next
action. When the blocking-finding rate is flat, the `needs-human` message says **split this
PR** and points at where — not "re-review".

## Scope / boundaries
**In scope**
- Recording per-round blocking-finding counts across the loop's rounds (the round dir under
  `<HARNESS_DIR>/.pr-loop/<pr>/round-<n>/` already exists as the natural home).
- Computing whether the rate is decaying or flat, and surfacing the series — the numbers, not
  a verdict dressed as one.
- Rewriting the cap-round `needs-human` outcome so a flat series recommends splitting, and
  including the per-file finding concentration already available in the fetched review JSON.
- Test coverage in `tests/test_pr_loop.sh`.

**Out of scope**
- Changing *when* the cap fires, or `max_rounds` / `blocking_severities` semantics.
- Auto-splitting anything. That is F04's territory and it needs a human decision.
- Auto-closing or auto-labelling beyond the existing `needs-human` label.

## Chosen options
- **Report the series, recommend the action.** The loop should not silently decide a PR is
  unsalvageable; it should show the twelve numbers that make the case. The operator who
  overrode the cap three times did so without ever seeing them aggregated.
- **Per-file concentration comes free.** Each review comment already carries `.path`; grouping
  by path is a `jq` line over JSON the loop has already fetched, and it converts "split this
  PR" from advice into a pointer. On PR #76 that grouping put 11 of 33 findings on one file
  that was 5.4% of the diff.
- **Trend, not threshold.** "More than N total findings" would have fired on a converging PR
  that simply had a lot to fix. Flatness across rounds is the property that actually
  distinguishes a non-converging review.

## Constraints
- **Blocked on E18-F01.** `.claude/commands/sdd-pr-loop.md`, `agents/pr-fixer.md` and
  `tests/test_pr_loop.sh` do not exist on `main`. Do not start this against a `main` checkout;
  do not vendor a copy of the command to work around it.
- The round cache is gitignored runtime scratch (`.pr-loop/` is already seeded into the
  consumer `.harness/.gitignore`). Trend data lives there and must stay rebuildable from the
  `gh` API — it is not board state and never becomes a source of truth.
- Preserve the existing clean-signal detection in all three of its forms (👍 reaction, summary
  banner, "didn't find any major issues" comment) and the `created_at >= trigger.created_at`
  freshness rule. A trend feature must not perturb how a clean round is recognised.
- The whole feature must stay inert when `pr_loop.enabled: false` — no glue stamped anywhere.
- Do not freeze an exact VERSION or diff DO-NOT-TOUCH files against `main` in any test added to
  `verification.test_command`.

## Open questions
- What counts as "flat". Least-squares slope over the series, or the simpler "the last K rounds
  each produced ≥1 blocking finding"? The simple rule is explainable in the `needs-human`
  message, which matters more here than statistical nicety.
- Whether the trend should also fire *before* the cap — e.g. at round 3 of 4, warning that the
  loop is on track to cap without converging. Probably yes; it is the difference between
  advice at hour 2 and advice at hour 4.
- How this interacts with a re-run of `/sdd-pr-loop` on the same PR: rounds are per-invocation
  today, but the trend is only meaningful across the PR's whole history.
