# Intent — pi model routing: `pin.pi.<tier>`, tier→thinking ladder, escalation arming

## Problem / who
ADR-0002 tiers and the builder-heavy escalation do not resolve on pi.

## Success outcome
`models.pin.pi.<tier>` (provider/id form, validated like opencode's) stamps `model:` into
`.pi/agents/<role>.md` and the dispatcher; a built-in tier→`thinking` ladder (mirroring
`codex_effort_alias`: e.g. reasoning→high, standard→medium, cheap→low; Architect decides
exact values) stamps `thinking:`; `escalation_verdict`/`write_escalation_arming` treat pi
like codex (an effort/thinking difference alone can arm). `pi_thinking` values validated
against `off minimal low medium high xhigh max`.

## Scope / boundaries
In scope: `resolve_model`, `model_alias`, `models_any`, `escalation_verdict`, config seed
comments (`harness.config.yaml` models block — remember the root config is the SEED
TEMPLATE), `migrate_config` copy, docs, `test_model_routing.sh`.
Out of scope: anything not routing.

## Constraints
- One resolver feeds both role files and `pi-dispatch.sh` (ADR-0007 consequence).
