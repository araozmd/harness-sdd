# Intent — Deprecate the stacked-PR lane and reclaim its machinery

## Why this exists

`E21-F06` asked one question before anything could be specced: **does the stacked-PR lane earn
its keep?** On 2026-08-17 the gate answered **no**. This feature is the honest follow-up the
F06 brief itself named.

The reasoning, recorded so it is not re-litigated:

- The **sibling-feature model already delivers budget-sized reviews** — E21's actual goal — with
  **zero new machinery** (`agents/driller.md:85-87`, `E21-F01` R4). An over-budget candidate is
  split into sibling features sequenced by `depends_on`, each its own board feature with its own
  PR and its own review.
- Stacking adds **exactly one thing** over that: wall-clock overlap.
- **Measured:** `0` of the repository's last `100` PRs targeted a non-default base. The three
  call sites of `tools/pr-stack-guard.sh` in the `/sdd-pr-loop` glue are all conditioned on
  `baseRefName != default_branch`, so the guard has **never fired**.
- The cost of keeping the lane alive is a delimiter convention, **four role-file contracts that
  must agree** (`architect`, `builder`, `orchestrator`, `reviewer`) plus their `.claude/agents/`
  pointer shims — the harness's most consistency-fragile surface, and the one that produced the
  most review rounds in recent memory.

## Problem / who

`docs/WORKFLOW.md` (accurate as of `E21-F05`, v0.63.1) documents a lane nobody uses, and the
installed body ships a merge-order guard with no live caller. An operator reading `WORKFLOW.md`
today is offered a mechanism the project has decided against, and a maintainer reading
`harness-install.sh` cannot tell that the guard is dead — a gate in a merge path reads as
load-bearing whether or not it is.

## Success outcome

`docs/WORKFLOW.md` states plainly that the lane is deprecated and names the sibling-feature model
as what to use instead; the guard and its glue are gone from the installed body; and nothing in
the harness still implies a stacked lane is available.

## Scope / boundaries

**In scope**
- `docs/WORKFLOW.md`: the lane section becomes a **deprecation notice** that names the
  sibling-feature model as the supported answer to an over-budget feature. Keep it short — a
  reader who arrives from an old link needs to know it is gone and where to go, not a history.
- **Remove `tools/pr-stack-guard.sh`** and its three call sites in the `/sdd-pr-loop` glue in
  `harness-install.sh`, plus the base-change-detection block that exists only to serve stacked
  children, plus its tests. Removal is deliberate over leaving it inert: a dead gate in a merge
  path is indistinguishable from a live one to the next reader, and git history preserves it if
  the decision is ever reversed.
- The `E21-F04` spec records that `E21-F07` supersedes it, append-only — F04 is `done` and its
  spec would otherwise read as describing shipped behavior that no longer exists.
- `VERSION` + `CHANGELOG.md`.

**Out of scope**
- The increment contract (`E21-F06`) — not to be built.
- Anything about how over-budget features are split; the sibling model is unchanged and this
  feature adds no rule to it.
- The rest of `/sdd-pr-loop`. Only the stacked-PR-specific blocks come out.

## Constraints

- **No atomicity language anywhere.** The predecessor spec was withdrawn over it after four
  review rounds.
- `tools/pr-stack-guard.sh`'s header states the doctrine in the banned denial form. It was
  DO-NOT-TOUCH while it shipped; deleting the file resolves that item rather than editing it.
- Removal must be **behavior-preserving in practice** and provable as such: the guard's call
  sites only execute when `baseRefName != default_branch`. A test must show that a PR targeting
  the default branch takes byte-identical decisions before and after. Do not assert this by
  diffing against `main`, which is banned here.
- A statement that must hold across files needs a **fence-aware** section extractor, and a
  possibly-zero `grep -c` must never be bound under `set -eu`. Both were shipped defects in the
  withdrawn E21-F05 spec.
- Do not freeze an exact `VERSION` string in any test.
- Must stay inert when `pr_loop.enabled: false`.
- Scoped to **~6 R-ids**. If it grows past 12, stop and report.

## Open questions

- **Does `docs/WORKFLOW.md` keep a deprecation notice permanently, or is the lane section deleted
  outright?** A notice helps a reader arriving from an old link or an old branch; a deletion
  leaves less to keep accurate. Recommend the notice, sized in sentences rather than sections.
- **Does removing an installed-body tool make this MINOR or MAJOR?** It removes no *capability
  a target relied on* (the guard never fired), but it does delete a file from every installed
  `.harness/tools/`. Read `AGENTS.md` → Versioning and justify rather than assume; the removal
  path in the installer must also reclaim the now-orphaned file from existing targets.
