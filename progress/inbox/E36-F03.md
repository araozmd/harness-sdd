# Intent — Product stage and deferred-decision ADRs with triggers; sticky vs bolt-on NFRs

## Problem / who
On MVP/pilot projects the Architect adds encryption at rest, WAF, rate limiting and
retries that no requirement asked for, because the project never says what stage it is in
and the model fills the gap with production defaults. Telling it "be minimal" is compliance
prose that ablates. ADR-0009 decisions 1–3 record the alternative: declare the stage once,
defer bolt-ons by writing an ADR with a trigger, never defer sticky NFRs. This feature ships
the declaration, the template and the role prose. Enforcement of citations is E36-F04.

## Success outcome
- `specs/product.md` accepts a frontmatter `stage: discovery | pilot | production`; absent ⇒
  `production`. There is no product.md template: the installer seeds the stub inline
  (`harness-install.sh`, "seeded specs/product.md (stub)") — that stub gains the field with
  a comment, and this repo's own `specs/product.md` declares `stage: production`.
- `specs/_templates/adr.md` documents a `Status: deferred` variant with a mandatory
  `Trigger:` line, and carries the sticky-NFR list (tenant isolation, auth boundary, secrets
  out of repo, validation at the system edge) with the sentence "never deferred at any
  stage", plus the bolt-on examples (encryption at rest, WAF, rate limits, backups, caching).
- `agents/planner.md`, `agents/driller.md`, `agents/architect.md` read the stage. The
  Architect, when the stage is `discovery` or `pilot`, writes or cites one deferred ADR per
  bolt-on it leaves out and cites it from the plan row or an explicit `## Deferred` plan
  subsection (added to `specs/_templates/feature.plan.md`); it never adds a bolt-on without
  an R-id. Doc-critic flags, the `init.sh` trigger sweep and the Reviewer gate belong to
  E36-F04, not here.
- Docs: a short "Stage and NFRs" section in docs/WORKFLOW.md with the sticky/bolt-on table.

## Scope / boundaries
In scope: product.md frontmatter (this repo + installer stub), adr template, plan template
`## Deferred` subsection, planner/driller/architect prose, docs, tests. Out of scope: the citation tool and the
Reviewer gate (E36-F04); any runtime security scanning; changing existing ADRs.

## Chosen options
- Stage lives in `specs/product.md` frontmatter (the repo has product.md, not vision.md).
- Deferred ADRs are ordinary ADRs with a status and a trigger, not a new artifact type.

## Constraints
- Honor ADR-0009. The sticky list is deliberately short; adding to it is a spec change, not
  an Architect judgment call.
- Projects without `stage` see no behavior change (backward compatible).

## Open questions
- None blocking; the `init.sh` sweep question moved to E36-F04 with the sweep.
