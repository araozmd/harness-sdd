## Why the split fell here

and none was disputed. **All three, and every role-file contradiction, live in the increment
contract** — who opens an increment's PR, how the Reviewer verifies a partial increment, how an

the doctrine burned through four review rounds; here the doctrine is sound and the contracts
burn. Cut where the findings concentrate.

## Decisions carried from the 2026-07-29 brief

Both survived Codex review untouched and are **not** to be re-derived:

1. **The stack is GROWN, not created up front.** Emitting N branches up front contradicts the
   Builder's own loop and requires knowing every seam before any code exists. The cost — a
   parent taking review fixes after a child exists needs a manual restack — is documented, not
   hidden.
2. **`done` rolls only when the last increment merges; no per-increment board record.** The
   board's unit is the feature. *(The mechanism for this is F06's; this brief carries only the
   doctrine sentence.)*

Decision 3 (the board does not represent a stack) is **under pressure** and belongs to F06 —
see that brief.
